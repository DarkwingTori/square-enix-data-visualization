## The Story Behind the Comparison

This isn't just a sales comparison — there's a real historical arc here:

- **Squaresoft and Nintendo were deeply intertwined** during the NES/SNES era. FF1-6, Chrono Trigger, Secret of Mana — all Nintendo exclusives
- **The break happened in 1996** when Square defected to PlayStation for FF7, largely because Sony's CD-ROM format allowed for FMV cutscenes and larger game worlds that cartridges couldn't support. Nintendo took it personally.
- **Square and Nintendo had a cold war** from 1997–2002. Square published nothing on Nintendo platforms during this period — not even re-releases
- **The reconciliation came in 2002** with FF: Crystal Chronicles on GameCube, ending the 5-year freeze
- **Post-merger Square Enix** became multiplatform, but Dragon Quest specifically remained heavily Nintendo-aligned (DQ9 and DQ11 on DS/3DS/Switch)

That backstory makes the Nintendo vs. PlayStation comparison one of the most interesting data stories in gaming history — and you'd be visualizing it.
