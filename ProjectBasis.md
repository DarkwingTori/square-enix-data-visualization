## What This Adds to the Project

**The Merger Story**
This becomes the emotional core of the whole visualization. You can show two separate timelines running in parallel — Squaresoft on one side, Enix on the other — then literally merge them into one stream. That's a genuinely compelling visual moment.

**Squaresoft-Specific Angles**
- Heavy on SNES and PS1 era JRPGs — Final Fantasy, Chrono Trigger, Secret of Mana, Vagrant Story, Xenogears
- Strong 2D → 3D transition story (FF1-6 vs FF7 onwards)
- Developer identity was very in-house during this era

**Enix-Specific Angles**
- Dragon Quest dominance, especially in Japan
- More publisher than developer — lots of third party titles to track
- Star Ocean, Valkyrie Profile (via tri-Ace), Neurotica, etc.
- Interesting JP vs. Western sales disparity story — Dragon Quest was massive in Japan but barely moved in the West pre-merger

**Cross-Era Comparisons**
- How did franchises change after the merger? FF, Dragon Quest, and Kingdom Hearts all straddle the divide
- Did the merger change genre diversity? Squaresoft was almost exclusively RPG; Square Enix broadened significantly
- Average Metacritic score by era — there's a popular fan narrative that quality dipped post-merger, your data could confirm or challenge that
